==========================
Django 6.1.1 release notes
==========================

*Expected September 2, 2026*

Django 6.1.1 fixes several bugs in 6.1.

Bugfixes
========

* Fixed a regression in Django 6.1 that caused a spurious
  ``RemovedInDjango70Warning`` about double-dot lookups when rendering
  templates containing string, translated, or numeric literals with two
  consecutive dots, such as ``{{ "a..b" }}`` (:ticket:`XXXXX`).
